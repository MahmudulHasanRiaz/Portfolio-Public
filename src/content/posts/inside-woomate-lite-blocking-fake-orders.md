Every WooCommerce store in Bangladesh knows the fake order: a name, a phone number nobody answers, and an address that goes nowhere. The order gets packed, the courier gets paid to carry it across the city — and paid again when it comes back unclaimed.

## Where the money leaks

Cash on delivery is how Bangladesh shops online, and it should stay that way — customers trust it. But COD means the store pays for delivery before it earns anything, so every fake order is a small, silent loss. Multiplied across a busy month, it quietly eats the margin on the real orders.

## What WooMate Lite does

WooMate Lite is the WordPress plugin I built for exactly this. It verifies orders at checkout and flags the patterns fake orders leave behind, so the store owner confirms before anything ships — the check happens at the one moment it costs nothing.

It is built specifically for how e-commerce works here: cash on delivery first, trust earned order by order. Genuine customers pass in seconds; the placed-and-vanished orders stop at the door.
