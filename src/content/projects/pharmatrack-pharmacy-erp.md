PharmaTrack ran on paper registers across eighteen branches: nobody knew what was expiring, what was out of stock, or what to order. Riaz built one Next.js ERP with batch and expiry alerts, branch-wise stock, supplier purchase orders and daily sales reports.

Managers see every branch from one dashboard, and pharmacists get warned before stock expires instead of after.
