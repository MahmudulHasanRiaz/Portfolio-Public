StyleNest ran a slow WooCommerce store with heavy cash-on-delivery fraud: fake orders, repeated numbers, and stock that never matched the shop floor. Riaz rebuilt the storefront for speed, added phone verification before dispatch, and blocked repeat offenders by phone, IP and device.

bKash and card checkout work side by side, and inventory syncs with the physical store so the website never sells what the shop does not have.
