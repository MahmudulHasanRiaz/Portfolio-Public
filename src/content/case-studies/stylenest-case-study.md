StyleNest sold mostly cash-on-delivery, and fake orders were eating the margin. Phone verification before dispatch and a blocklist by phone, IP and device cut fake COD orders by 71 percent in the first month — and the store survived an Eid flash sale at three times normal traffic without slowing down.
